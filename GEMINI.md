# BenchFleet

BenchFleet is a self-hosted control hub for real Android phones, for people and AI agents. It is a desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux: Claude Code, Codex or any MCP client can use the phones you choose, with approvals, budgets and a hash-chained audit log. App teams record a test once and run it on their own phones after every build, and anyone can see every phone live, take control with mouse and keyboard, and automate the work with workflows and schedules. Local mode sends no telemetry, and the Free plan covers 3 phones.
